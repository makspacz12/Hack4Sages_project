Concept: using AMUSE in this project (draft)
--------------------------------------------

Main goal:
    - Extend the current simulation (REBOUND/REBOUNDx) with additional physics
      that may in future require several specialised solvers.

What we already have without AMUSE:
    - N-body dynamics in REBOUND: the Sun + planets + 50 Gaia stars.
    - (Planned) radiation pressure on asteroids via REBOUNDx.
    - The ability to add "local" per-asteroid processes (cosmic radiation,
      hydrolysis, erosion) as our own modules on top of REBOUND.

Potential roles for AMUSE:
1) Managing multiple solvers:
    - N-body: different gravitational codes (ph4, Huayno, Hermite) instead of or
      alongside REBOUND.
    - Hydrodynamics: gas models after an explosion (SPH) and their effect on
      asteroid motion.
    - Stellar evolution: L(t), M(t) changes for stars (e.g. SSE/BSE), feeding
      beta(t) in the radiation pressure model.
    - Coupling: AMUSE ties these solvers together and handles data exchange over time.

2) Consistent units and time:
    - AMUSE has a built-in unit system (amuse.units), which helps:
        - avoid conversion errors (AU vs m vs pc),
        - integrate different codes (each may use different internal units).
    - It can take over global time control and the steps between solvers.

3) Advanced scenarios:
    - Modelling the evolution of the whole local stellar environment
      (changing stellar masses and luminosities, motion in the galactic potential).
    - Adding an external galactic potential (galactic plane, spiral arms) affecting
      asteroid and stellar trajectories.
    - A joint simulation of:
        - a gas cloud after an explosion (hydro),
        - asteroids inside that gas (N-body),
        - evolving stars (evolution codes),
      all synchronised through AMUSE.

4) Comparative experiments:
    - Easy comparison of:
        - the same problem solved with different N-body solvers,
        - the effect of different stellar evolution or hydrodynamics models on
          asteroid survival.

When it is actually worth moving to AMUSE:
    - Once the project grows beyond "REBOUND + local particle physics" and we start
      needing:
        - full hydrodynamics of a post-explosion cloud,
        - realistic stellar evolution (variable L(t), M(t)),
        - a strongly coupled multi-physics simulation with several solvers.
    - For now REBOUND/REBOUNDx is sufficient; AMUSE is treated as a possible
      stage 2.0 for a more complex, multi-physics version of the project.
