Concept: using ATM (Asteroid Thermal Modeling) in this project
--------------------------------------------------------------

Role of ATM:
    - Provide realistic surface and subsurface temperature models for asteroids
      as a function of:
        * distance from the Sun (r),
        * size (R),
        * albedo, thermal inertia, rotation and shape.
    - ATM results (T(theta, phi, t) or averaged T) would feed into:
        * hydrolysis and chemical reaction models,
        * erosion / sublimation models (change of radius and mass),
        * assessment of life survivability in different parts of an asteroid.

Integration with REBOUND/REBOUNDx:
    - REBOUND/REBOUNDx:
        * computes the orbits of the Sun, planets, 50 stars and asteroids
          (N-body + radiation pressure),
        * gives us asteroid trajectories: r(t), v(t).
    - ATM:
        * is not called on every REBOUND step,
        * runs "above" the simulation:
            - for representative orbit points (or for a grid in (r, R, parameters)),
            - computes T for a given asteroid class,
        * results are tabulated (lookup tables) and later interpolated in time.

Typical cycle:
    1. Generate an asteroid population (R, rho, albedo, rotation, material).
    2. For a grid of (r, R, material parameters) run ATM and store:
         - mean surface and subsurface temperatures,
         - optionally the temperature ranges.
    3. During a long REBOUND integration:
         - every larger step (e.g. every 10^4-10^5 years) for each asteroid:
             a) take its current r (and other parameters),
             b) interpolate T from the ATM tables,
             c) update:
                 - hydrolysis / chemical reaction rates,
                 - erosion/sublimation rate (-> dR/dt, change of mass and beta(t)),
                 - the life survivability indicator eta(t).

Why ATM:
    - Instead of hand-made T(r) models we use a code validated against data
      (NEOWISE and similar).
    - It separates the "heavy" thermal physics from the N-body layer:
        * REBOUND: trajectories,
        * ATM: trustworthy temperatures,
        * our module: chemistry/biology and the evolution of radius and beta.

When to implement ATM:
    - After stabilising:
        * the orbital model (REBOUND/REBOUNDx),
        * the basic radius/beta evolution without full thermal physics.
    - ATM would be a stage 2.0 for a more realistic description of temperatures and
      of the temperature-dependent processes on and inside asteroids.
