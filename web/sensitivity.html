Morris elementary-effects screening

Every factor is moved, many times, from many different starting points in the parameter space. μ* is the mean size of the resulting change in median survival — how much a factor matters. σ is how much that influence itself varies depending on where you are — which is non-linearity, or interaction with another factor. The two have to be read together, which is why they are the two axes of one plot rather than a ranked bar chart.

Heatmap →

How to read it

  • Near the origin — negligible. The factor can be frozen.
  • Right, and low (σ/μ* < 0.1) — influential, and linear and additive: its effect is the same wherever you are in the space.
  • Right, and high (σ/μ* > 1) — influential, but non-linear or interacting: how much it matters depends on every other setting, and no single number describes it.
  • Filled circle — the mean effect clears Morris's ±2·SEM wedge. Hollow — it does not, and the effect may be indistinguishable from zero at this number of trajectories.
  • A violet connector — |μ| is smaller than μ*, so the factor pushes one way in part of the space and the other way elsewhere. A signed ranking would have cancelled it to nothing.

Why this replaced a tornado chart

The page that stood here drew a one-at-a-time tornado: each knob shifted ±10% while everything else was held at baseline. That design is not merely older. It measures a local derivative at one point, it cannot detect an interaction even in principle, and over eight factors it reaches 1.59% of the input space.

Its shipped sample was also broken in ways worth stating plainly, because they are on record: the run lasted half a year with four fragments, the largest swing it measured was 2.0×10⁻⁷, two of the eight knobs moved the result by exactly zero, and its baseline for the radiation coefficient was 3.16×10⁻⁶ 1/Gy — a factor of 7.9 below the floor of this model's own published band. Keeping it behind a caveat would have been worse than removing it.

  • Campolongo, F., Cariboni, J. & Saltelli, A. (2007). An effective screening design for sensitivity analysis of large models. Environmental Modelling & Software 22:1509–1518. μ*, the mean of absolute effects, which does not cancel for a non-monotonic factor.
